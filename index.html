How to read this dashboard · definitions, scope & limitations
  • Source: 192 publicly listed videos from DevoxxForever’s Videos tab, published from October 1, 2026 through the October 20 extraction. No qualifying uploads after October 13 were found. Shorts, Live, and unavailable uploads are excluded.
  • Snapshot: exact watch-page views and public likes captured October 20, 2026, approximately 14:18–14:21 UTC. Counts are embedded locally and do not update. Individual capture times are included in CSV exports.
  • Publication dates: calendar dates from YouTube’s supplied publication timestamps (UTC−07:00), not event/session dates. The date interval is inclusive.
  • Like rate: likes ÷ views × 100. Aggregate rates are view-weighted (sum of likes ÷ sum of views), not averages of percentages. Likes are not unique viewers or a measure of watch time.
  • * Lifetime views/day: views divided by exact elapsed days between publication and each video’s capture timestamp. This is an age-normalized lifetime average, not current daily views, growth, or velocity. Older and newer videos still differ in exposure and distribution.
  • Concentration: top-decile share uses the highest-viewed ceil(10% × selected count) videos. Quartiles use linear interpolation; median is the middle value (or mean of the two middle values).
  • Topics: transparent, overlapping keyword rules on the title before an apparent “by” speaker credit. Expand the topic rules below to inspect them. Unmatched titles are “Other / unclassified.” Topic comparisons are descriptive, not causal.
  • Limits: no impressions, click-through rate, watch time, subscriber conversions, audience demographics, traffic sources, or historical snapshots. We cannot infer content quality, changes over time, or that title wording caused performance. All metrics and insights recompute within your current selection.
