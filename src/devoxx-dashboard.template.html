How to read this dashboard · definitions, scope & limitations
  • Source: 192 publicly listed videos from DevoxxForever’s Videos tab, published from October 1, 2026 through the October 20 extraction. No qualifying uploads after October 13 were found. Shorts, Live, and unavailable uploads are excluded.
  • Snapshot: exact watch-page views and public likes captured October 20, 2026, approximately 14:18–14:21 UTC. Counts are embedded locally and do not update. Individual capture times are included in CSV exports.
  • Comments: collected separately from the public watch-page comments header, resolving its initial comments continuation when needed. Exact integer counts only; abbreviated/missing counts are unavailable, disabled comments are marked disabled, and request/parser failures are marked failed. None is assumed to mean zero. Each record retains comment count status, reported label, source, error (if any), and a separate UTC collection timestamp. No comment text or authors are included.
  • Speaker benchmarks: all 192 videos by default, regardless of title search. You may explicitly switch to the current dashboard filters. The independent talk selector never changes the comparison population. A selected talk outside the cohort is still compared with that cohort and is marked as outside it. Comments exclude null counts and show the smaller sample size; genuine zeros are included.
  • Percentiles: P50/P90/P95/P99 are count thresholds using ascending values and linear interpolation at index (n − 1) × p (the same as inclusive spreadsheet percentiles). Fractional thresholds are not observed integer counts. Percentile rank = 100 × number of comparison values ≤ the talk's value ÷ n; ties receive the same upper cumulative rank. The empirical CDF is a step curve. Empty populations yield no thresholds/ranks; a single value yields that value for every threshold. High ranks describe public counts, not talk quality.
  • Publication dates: calendar dates from YouTube’s supplied publication timestamps (UTC−07:00), not event/session dates. The date interval is inclusive.
  • Like rate: likes ÷ views × 100. Aggregate rates are view-weighted (sum of likes ÷ sum of views), not averages of percentages. Likes are not unique viewers or a measure of watch time.
  • * Lifetime views/day: views divided by exact elapsed days between publication and each video’s capture timestamp. This is an age-normalized lifetime average, not current daily views, growth, or velocity. Older and newer videos still differ in exposure and distribution.
  • Concentration: top-decile share uses the highest-viewed ceil(10% × selected count) videos. Quartiles use linear interpolation; median is the middle value (or mean of the two middle values).
  • Topics: transparent, overlapping keyword rules on the title before an apparent “by” speaker credit. Expand the topic rules below to inspect them. Unmatched titles are “Other / unclassified.” Topic comparisons are descriptive, not causal.
  • Limits: no impressions, click-through rate, watch time, subscriber conversions, audience demographics, traffic sources, or historical snapshots. We cannot infer content quality, changes over time, or that title wording caused performance. Dashboard metrics and insights follow your filters; speaker benchmarks use their separately selected comparison population.
